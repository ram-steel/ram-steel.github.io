About Our Company

RAM Engineering for Steel Structure

We take this opportunity to introduce ourselves as one of the leading Mechanical & Structural fabrication & Erection engineers in Egypt. Developing a fully-equipped facility extending over our factory equipped with modern and advanced technological facilities for the manufacturing & testing of steel structures with a capacity of 1,400 tons per year. Our Journey

Our Mission & Organizational Growth

Our mission is to provide high-quality service products combined with trusted client partnerships, and to maintain high health and safety levels and engineered solutions tailored to client needs.

2021

Company Founding

Established RAM Engineering for Steel Structure & General Supplies in Khanka, Qalyubia, starting commercial and industrial operations.

2021

Official Registrations

Officially registered under Commercial Register No. 44673 and Tax Registration No. 625-771-494 with legal status fully compliance.

2022

E-Invoicing System Integration

Integrated with the Egyptian Tax Authority digital E-Invoicing system to maintain complete legal and financial transparency.

2023

1,400 Tons Production Capacity

Expanded our fabrication workshop facility equipped with advanced machinery to reach a production capacity of 1,400 tons per year.

2024

Key Client Partnerships

Executed major structural steel projects for Oriental Weavers, El Marakby Steel, El Sewedy Cables, and Arcosteel.

2025

MEP Systems Integration

Broadened operations to offer turnkey MEP solutions, including Firefighting, Plumbing, and HVAC systems.
